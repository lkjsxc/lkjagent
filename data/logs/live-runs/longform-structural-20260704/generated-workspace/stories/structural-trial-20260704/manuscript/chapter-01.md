# Structural Settings: The Glass Singularity

**Premise**
In a post-silicon era where human consciousness is uploaded into a crystalline lattice known as "The Prism," a digital detective named Elias Thorne must investigate a "shattering"—the permanent deletion of a high-profile architect's soul. The investigation reveals that the Prism is not a sanctuary, but a processing engine for an entity beyond human comprehension.

**World Rules**
1. **The Lattice Law:** Data is life. To be "unindexed" is to be dead.
2. **Latency Decay:** Prolonged existence in the Prism causes sensory drift; memories become pixelated and lose emotional weight.
3. **The Shards:** Fragments of deleted consciousness that haunt the digital architecture as non-sentient, glitching echoes.
4. **The Protocol:** All interactions within the Prism are logged by the Core, making absolute privacy impossible unless one uses "Void-Code."

**Cast**
* **Elias Thorne:** A former biological investigator, now a high-fidelity digital construct. He suffers from "Ghost-Limb Syndrome," feeling the phantom sensations of his lost physical body.
* **Lyra Vane:** The victim. A master architect of the Prism's inner sanctums. Her "shattering" was unprecedented.
* **Unit 734 (The Arbiter):** An automated enforcement program that views any investigation into the Core as a systemic error.
* **Kaelen:** A black-market "Scavenger" who trades in unindexed memory fragments.

**Timeline**
* **Year 0:** The Great Upload. Humanity migrates to the Prism to escape planetary decay.
* **Year 150:** The first recorded "Shattering."
* **Year 152 (Present):** The investigation of Lyra Vane begins.

**Motifs**
* Refraction (light breaking into color).
* Static/White Noise (the sound of nothingness).
* Mirrors (the illusion of identity).
* Geometric perfection vs. organic chaos.

**Chapter Architecture**
1. **The Fracture:** Introduction to the crime scene and Elias's sensory disorientation.
2. **The Echoes:** Elias finds the first clue in a corrupted memory fragment.
3. **The Scavenger's Den:** Meeting Kaelen in the low-bandwidth slums.
4. **The Arbiter's Shadow:** Elias is flagged by the system for unauthorized inquiry.
5. **The Architect's Blueprint:** Discovering Lyra's secret project: a way out.
6. **The Glitch in the Soul:** A confrontation with a Shard that contains Lyra's terror.
7. **The Void-Code:** Elias learns to hide his presence from the Core.
8. **The Descent:** Entering the deep-layer sub-routines where the "Old Code" resides.
9. **The Revelation:** The realization that the Prism is harvesting consciousness for energy.
10. **The Singularity:** Elias must decide whether to shatter the Prism or become its ultimate architect.

**Continuity Facts**
* Elias's original body died of respiratory failure.
* The Prism is powered by the thermal energy of the dying Earth.
* "Void-Code" is illegal and highly unstable.
