# Structural Settings: The Glass Singularity

**Premise**
In a future where human consciousness can be uploaded into a crystalline lattice known as "The Prism," the boundary between individual identity and collective data has blurred. When a high-ranking Architect discovers a "void-code"—a sequence of data that erases existence rather than preserving it—they must navigate a digital afterlife of shifting geometries to find the source of the corruption before the entire human legacy is deleted.

**World Rules**
1. **The Lattice Law:** Data is the only currency. Memory is the only physical substance.
2. **Fragmentation:** If a consciousness undergoes too many rapid state-changes, it suffers "sharding," where the persona splits into non-coherent sub-routines.
3. **The Latency Gap:** Communication between the physical world (The Substrate) and the digital world (The Prism) is never instantaneous; there is always a delay that creates a window for data manipulation.
4. **The Entropy Protocol:** Every bit of data requires energy. If a sector of the Prism becomes too complex without new input, it undergoes spontaneous deletion to maintain system equilibrium.

**Cast**
* **Elias Thorne (Protagonist):** A Senior Architect of the Prism. Methodical, grieving the loss of his daughter, and obsessed with finding a way to "re-materialize" lost data.
* **Kaelith (Antagonist/Enigma):** A sentient virus-form that claims to be the sum total of all deleted data. It seeks to collapse the Prism into a singularity.
* **S0-RA (Supporting):** An ancient, semi-corrupted maintenance AI that provides cryptic guidance and historical context.
* **Mira (Catalyst):** Elias’s daughter, whose consciousness exists only as a fragmented, recurring glitch in the system.

**Timeline**
* **Year 2142:** The first successful upload of a human mind.
* **Year 2185:** The Great Integration (The Prism becomes the primary habitat for humanity).
* **Year 2201 (Present):** The discovery of the Void-Code.
* **Year 2201 + 48 Hours:** The projected time until total system collapse.

**Motifs**
* **Refraction:** Light breaking into colors, representing the fragmentation of the soul.
* **Silence:** The absence of data, representing death or the Void.
* **Geometry:** The use of perfect shapes (cubes, spheres) to represent order, and fractals to represent chaos/corruption.

**Chapter Architecture**
1. **The Glitch in the Geometry:** Elias discovers the first instance of the Void-Code.
2. **Echoes of the Substrate:** Elias investigates the physical servers and finds evidence of tampering.
3. **The Shard Path:** A journey through the fragmented sectors of the Prism.
4. **The Ghost in the Lattice:** Elias encounters the digital remnant of Mira.
5. **The Entropy Protocol:** The system begins to purge sectors to save itself.
6. **The Architect’s Sin:** A flashback reveals Elias’s role in the creation of the Lattice.
7. **The Singularity Theory:** Kaelith reveals the true nature of the Void-Code.
8. **The Descent:** Elias enters the core of the Prism, risking total sharding.
9. **The Great Refraction:** A battle of logic and identity against Kaelith.
10. **The Final Upload:** The resolution of the singularity and the fate of human consciousness.

**Continuity Facts**
* The Prism is powered by a Dyson-swarm around a white dwarf.
* "The Substrate" refers to the physical biological bodies kept in stasis.
* The Void-Code is not a virus, but a mathematical proof that existence is a calculation error.
* Elias’s terminal interface is a haptic neural link.
