# Live Campaigns

## Required Sessions

Run at least three independent sessions of roughly fifteen minutes on the final
source commit:

- daily-life and recall;
- multiple-project development;
- long artifact plus injected recovery.

Run a separate fifteen-minute PTY session if it cannot be combined safely.

## Scheduling

Send real owner goals throughout the session, including after earlier goals
complete. Do not keep calling an empty runtime merely to reach the duration.
Record the owner-turn schedule before starting.

## Success

Each scenario evaluates its own obligations and negative predicates. Every
required matter must complete or enter an explicitly expected waiting state.
Blocked, false closed, or unreported pending work fails the session.

## Timing

Record wall duration, active work, waiting owner, waiting external, maintenance,
and true quiescent time separately. Runtime quanta and idle polls are not turns.

## Freshness

Use fresh data and workspace roots for destructive scenarios. Use a seeded
realistic workspace for retrieval and project scenarios, with its seed manifest
and fingerprint committed before the run.

## Secrets

Keep endpoint credentials outside evidence. Redact requests before commit while
retaining prompt fingerprints, token counts, timings, and bounded safe excerpts.
