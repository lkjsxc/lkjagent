# Adoption Ledger

## Entry

For every tested idea or combination, record:

- hypothesis;
- exact factors and configuration;
- scenarios and repeats;
- metrics and raw evidence paths;
- improvements;
- regressions;
- interactions with other ideas;
- decision: adopt, reject, or conditional;
- scope and follow-up.

## Rules

- No idea is adopted from design appeal alone.
- A standalone loss may remain conditional if a measured combination wins.
- Rejected code leaves product source after its evidence is retained.
- An adopted change receives focused regression tests.
- Default configuration uses only adopted behavior.

## Required Comparisons

Include context combinations, action grammar, tool-view sizes, constrained
output, recovery ladders, long-work splitting, and TUI event architecture.

## Final Summary

The handoff lists adopted defaults and conditional features. It does not hide
failed experiments or claim that all ideas helped.
