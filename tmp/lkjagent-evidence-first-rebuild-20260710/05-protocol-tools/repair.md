# Repair

## Parse Repair

First repair names the exact fault and renders the allowed grammar with current
decision values. It excludes failed raw output except a small escaped excerpt
hash. A second failure narrows the grammar or enables endpoint constraints.

## Admission Repair

State the rejected field, primitive rule, current allowed tools, and fixed
harness-selected arguments. Do not redisplay unrelated tools.

## Semantic Repair

A valid action that makes no objective progress triggers inspect or replan, not
format retry. A repeated read may be admitted only after a source fingerprint or
information need changes.

## Budget Repair

Output-limit faults split semantic units or continue from a safe committed
boundary. They never repeat an impossible target and cap unchanged.

## Escalation

After the typed ladder, select a deterministic fallback, owner clarification, or
visible waiting-external state. Do not convert failure into synthetic idle.
