# Evaluation Operator

## Objective

Build reproducible evidence that cannot confuse idle, blocked, and completed.

## Work

- preserve redacted current failure fixtures;
- replace elapsed-loop profiles with scenario predicates;
- prepare realistic personal and project seeds;
- run context, grammar, tool, recovery, and planning combinations;
- schedule real goals throughout repeated fifteen-minute runs;
- collect raw SQLite, workspace, trace, prompt, tool, check, and timing evidence;
- redact secrets without removing mechanical facts.

## Rules

Use fresh data, bind evidence to commit, stop each matter at its real terminal
state, and classify blocked as failed. Idle polls are not turns.

## Output

Produce raw-first evidence directories and adoption ledger. Do not author the
independent pass result.
